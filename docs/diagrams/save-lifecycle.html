The unsaved-changes lifecycle

The bar at the bottom of the card is a view of four states. Clean and Dirty are not stored anywhere: they follow from the change count.

What counts as one change

Each level that differs from the snapshot, a rename, an added role and a removed role. Undoing an edit by hand lowers the count, and at zero the card is Clean again.

During Saving

Discard is disabled and extra Save clicks are ignored. The rails stay live; an edit made in those 650 ms is not in this save and shows as unsaved afterwards.

Saved

A green tick draws itself and the bar leaves after 1.7 s.
